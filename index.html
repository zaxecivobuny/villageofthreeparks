Village of Three Parks

The Village of Three Parks has been in existence for over 50 years. This neighborhood, located in unincorporated north St. Louis County, is comprised of the following streets:

  • Grants Pkwy
  • Chesswood Dr
  • Shadowcreek Dr
  • Dawnview Dr
  • Dawnview Ct (aka Dawnview Pointe Dr.)

This also includes Sherwood Dr from Sherwood Ct to Wiethaupt, including Elksforth Ct.
